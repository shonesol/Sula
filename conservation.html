Conservation

Travel that respects wildlife, habitats, and local communities.

Our approach

Chichi Safaris believes tourism should fund protection, not pressure. We prioritise licensed operators, park fees that support conservation, and lodges with strong community links.

What we do

  • Keep groups small to reduce impact on sensitive areas
  • Follow park distance and behaviour rules around wildlife
  • Work with local guides and suppliers whenever possible
  • Share practical tips so guests travel responsibly

Travel with purpose

When you book with us, your trip contributes to park systems and local livelihoods. Ask us about community visits or conservation projects linked to your route.
