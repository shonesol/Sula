Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about travelling with Chichi Safaris.

When is the best time to visit?

East Africa is rewarding year-round. Dry seasons (roughly June–October and January–February) often mean easier game viewing. We tailor timing to migration, gorilla permits, and your preferences.

Are your trips suitable for families?

Yes. We design private itineraries for families, including child-friendly lodges and flexible game-drive lengths. Tell us ages when you enquire.

How do I pay?

After we agree an itinerary, we share payment options (bank transfer and other methods depending on your location). A deposit secures lodges and park fees.

Do I need visas and vaccines?

Requirements vary by nationality and destination. Check official government sources. Yellow fever certificates are often requested; malaria prophylaxis is commonly advised.

How do I book?

Use our booking form — it opens WhatsApp with your details to +256 702 094 292. Or email chichisafaris@gmail.com.
