About Chichi Safaris

Born from a deep love for Africa’s landscapes and the creatures that call them home.

Our Story

Chichi Safaris was founded in Jinja, Uganda, with a simple mission: to share authentic African wildlife experiences while protecting the places and communities that make them possible.

From the open plains of the Serengeti to the roar of Murchison Falls, our expert local guides open doors to journeys that feel personal, respectful, and unforgettable.

What We Stand For

  • Local expert guides who know the land and its wildlife
  • Small-group and private safari options
  • Conservation-focused travel
  • Comfortable camps and lodges with authentic character

Why Travel With Us

Every itinerary is carefully crafted to balance adventure, comfort, and genuine connection with nature. We work closely with parks, community lodges, and conservation partners across East Africa.
