Privacy Policy

How Chichi Safaris collects and uses your information.

Terms Privacy FAQ Contact

Information we collect

When you enquire or book, we may collect your name, email, phone number, travel dates, group size, and message content. Booking enquiries sent via WhatsApp are handled on that platform under WhatsApp’s own policies.

How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and prepare itineraries
  • To confirm bookings and share travel logistics
  • To improve our services

Sharing

We may share necessary details with lodges, parks, and transport partners solely to deliver your safari. We do not sell your personal data.

Admin & website data

Safari itineraries and gallery media are stored using Firebase (Firestore) and Cloudinary. Access to the admin area is restricted to authorised staff.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at chichisafaris@gmail.com or via our contact page.

Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page.
